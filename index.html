About Us

Creating. Celebrating. Inspiring

Abhay Productions has been running in Visakhapatnam for over 18 years, founded by T. Dhanunjay, a Vizag businessman who has run Dhanunjay Awards & Gifts for 29+ years, out of a personal passion for movies and the artists behind them. Since then, Abhay Productions has staged events, made films, and produced cover songs — giving recognition to countless artists across Vizag along the way. That passion still drives every project today.
