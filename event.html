Key Moments

How The Night Unfolded.

6:00 PM

Doors Open

Guests were welcomed with live folk music as they arrived at Sri Kanti Theatre.

6:45 PM

Opening Ceremony

The evening began with the traditional lighting of the lamp by our guests of honour.

7:30 PM

Guest Address

A special address celebrating the cultural significance of Telangana’s folk art.

8:15 PM

Folk Dance Showcase

Troupes from across the region took the stage in a dazzling display of tradition.

9:30 PM

Grand Finale

A drum-led performance brought the audience to its feet for the night’s biggest ovation.

10:00 PM

Closing Remarks

A heartfelt thank-you to every performer, partner, and guest who made the night unforgettable.
